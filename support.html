Restoring your subscription

If you've reinstalled Niyyah or switched to a new iPhone, open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Niyyah. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

Adding a widget

Widgets are part of Niyyah Pro. In the app, open your profile and tap Widgets for a step-by-step guide to adding one to your Home Screen.

Prayer times look wrong

Niyyah needs location access to calculate prayer times for where you are. Check that location is allowed for Niyyah in your iPhone's Settings. You can also change the calculation method under Reminders in your profile, if your local mosque follows a different one.
